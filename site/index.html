Encryption, identity, access, exposure, AI.

The secure option at every decision.

AI assistants can ship services on plain HTTP, leave admin consoles and AI tools on default credentials, and keep default settings unless told otherwise. SecureConfig gives your assistant short, copy-ready guides that make the secure option the default at each deployment decision. Guides include checks your assistant runs and reports, including what it could not verify.

What it gives you

An AI assistant that sets up a service does not always close every open door unless it is told to: encryption left off, default credentials for logins, factory settings kept. SecureConfig gives it a guide for each of those decisions, drawn from the vendors' documentation and free to use under CC0, so the secure setting goes in the first time. Guides include checks that report what passed, what failed, and what could not be verified. The checklist's steps are reasoned from cited vendor sources, with no deployment run recorded, and most guides record the versions they were written against.
