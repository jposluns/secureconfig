Guide quality

The Guide Quality Standard

The Guide Quality Standard (QUALITY.md (opens in a new tab)) scores every guide against thirty criteria in ten groups, from the mechanical floor to coverage, honesty, version basis and readability. This page renders the matrix; the scored source is tools/guide_quality.tsv (opens in a new tab) and the long-form findings are in tools/guide_quality_ledger.tsv (opens in a new tab).

Scope

A cell holds the worst level in its group followed by the ids of every criterion not at PASS: P, PARTIAL(A3), GAP(A2) PARTIAL(A3), ? for a criterion not yet scored, or n/a. A ? counts as PARTIAL and blocks EXEMPLARY, so a guide reaches EXEMPLARY only once a second reader has scored every cell to PASS or n/a.

The judgment criteria are scored by two readers by hand; this build fills the tool-computed cells and leaves every judgment cell at ?. The verdict column is the rule computation from the ten cells (QUALITY.md 3.3), and the guide-quality gate holds the matrix well-formed and each row bound to the guide it scored.
